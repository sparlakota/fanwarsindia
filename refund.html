Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Fan Power contributions on FanWarsIndia are a digital service: the moment your payment is confirmed, your contribution is added to the leaderboards and your Fan Card is created. Because of that, this policy is simple.

1. Cancellation

You can cancel any time before completing payment — just close the payment window and nothing is charged. Once a payment is confirmed, the contribution cannot be cancelled.

2. Contributions are non-refundable

Confirmed contributions are voluntary and are not refundable, including if you change your mind, back a different star later, or your star's leaderboard position changes.

3. When we will refund

  • Money deducted but contribution not shown. If your bank or UPI app shows a deduction but the payment wasn't confirmed to us, it is normally reversed automatically by your bank or our payment partner within 5–7 working days. If it isn't, contact us and we'll sort it out.
  • Charged twice. If you were charged more than once for the same contribution, we refund the extra payment.
  • Wrong amount due to a technical error on our side — we refund the difference.
  • Unauthorised payment. If a payment was made from your account without your permission, report it to us (and your bank) as soon as possible. We'll investigate and, if confirmed, refund it.

When a contribution is refunded, it is removed from the leaderboards.

4. How to request a refund

Contact us within 30 days of the payment at support@fanwarsindia.com with:

  • your Fan Card ID (looks like FW-XXXXXX) if you have it,
  • the mobile number you used,
  • the amount, date and time of the payment, and
  • the bank or UPI transaction reference, if available.

5. Refund timelines

We respond to refund requests within 2 working days. Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 3–15 working days, depending on your bank and payment method.

6. Questions

See our Contact page.
