Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data FanWarsIndia (fanwarsindia.com, operated by Shabareesh Parlakota) collects, why, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We collect as little as we can.

1. What we collect

  • Your name — the name you type when you support a star.
  • Your mobile number — used as your fan identity so each person is counted once in Fan Army and Top Fans. We don't send you SMS or marketing.
  • Contribution details — the star you supported, the amount, the time, your Fan Card ID and payment references from our payment partner.
  • A scrambled version of your IP address — a one-way, salted hash (the real IP address is not stored in our database), used only to stop automated spam of our payment system.

We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Those go directly to our payment partner, Dodo Payments.

2. What is shown publicly

When your payment succeeds, the following appear on the public leaderboards and Fan Activity feed: your name, the star you backed, the amount, the time, and only the last 4 digits of your mobile number (so two fans with the same name can be told apart). Your full mobile number is never shown publicly.

3. Why we use it

  • To process your contribution and confirm it with our payment partner.
  • To count unique fans and run the leaderboards.
  • To create your Fan Card.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and spam, and to keep records we are legally required to keep.
  • To respond if you contact us.

By entering your details and making a contribution, you consent to this use. You can withdraw consent for future use at any time (see section 6).

4. Who we share it with

  • Dodo Payments — processes your payment; receives the amount and our order reference, plus the details you enter on its checkout (such as your email and payment method). We do not send it your mobile number.
  • Supabase — hosts our database and server; your data is stored in their Mumbai, India data centre.
  • Vercel — hosts the website.
  • The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from Tailwind CSS and Dodo Payments (the checkout). Like any website, these providers and our hosts receive your IP address and browser details when you visit, and may keep short-lived technical logs.
  • Government or law-enforcement authorities, only where the law requires it.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep contribution records for as long as needed to run the leaderboards and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements, which can require keeping payment records for several years. If you ask, we will remove your name from public display even where we must keep the underlying payment record.

6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or update it;
  • ask us to erase it, subject to records the law requires us to keep;
  • withdraw your consent for future use;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf;
  • raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer (below) from the mobile number or with the Fan Card ID you used, so we can confirm it's you.

7. Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database is not publicly readable; only our own server functions can access contribution records, and phone numbers are never sent to anyone's browser. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. Children

FanWarsIndia is meant for users aged 18 and over. Anyone younger should only use it with a parent's or guardian's consent.
